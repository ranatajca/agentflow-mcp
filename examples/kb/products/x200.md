# X200 professional cleaning robot

## Overview
The X200 is built for offices and small warehouses: 4 hour runtime, LiDAR mapping and a 3 litre bin.

## Pricing
List price is 1,899 USD. The X200 Pro with the auto-empty dock is 2,349 USD.

## Common replacement parts
Main brush assembly: 64 USD. Drive wheel module: 189 USD each. LiDAR sensor: 412 USD.
Battery pack (5200 mAh): 129 USD.

## Known issues
Firmware before 3.2 can lose the floor map after a power cut. Updating fixes it; no part replacement needed.
