# R5 home cleaning robot

## Overview
The R5 is a home robot with 2.5 hour runtime, camera navigation and a 0.6 litre bin. The R5 Mini is a compact variant.

## Pricing
List price is 649 USD. The R5 Mini is 449 USD.

## Common replacement parts
Side brush pair: 18 USD. Drive wheel module: 74 USD each. Camera module: 139 USD.
Battery pack (3200 mAh): 59 USD.
