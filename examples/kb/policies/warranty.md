# Warranty policy

## Coverage periods
Consumer robots (the R5 and R5 Mini) carry a 12 month limited warranty from the delivery date.
Professional robots (the X200 and X200 Pro) carry a 24 month limited warranty from the delivery date.
Batteries on every model are covered for 6 months regardless of the robot's warranty period.

## What is covered
Manufacturing defects in motors, sensors, the main board and the charging dock.
Accidental damage, water damage and normal wear of brushes and wheels are not covered.

## Extended care
Extended Care adds 12 months of coverage and must be purchased within 30 days of delivery.
It costs 15% of the robot's list price.

## Out-of-warranty repairs
Out-of-warranty repairs are billed as a flat diagnostic fee of 79 USD plus parts.
Customers in the Loyalty program receive 20% off the parts cost, not the diagnostic fee.
