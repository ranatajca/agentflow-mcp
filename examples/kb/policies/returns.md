# Returns and refunds

## Return window
Any robot can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if it is undamaged and in its original packaging.

## Restocking fee
Opened professional robots returned between day 15 and day 30 incur a 10% restocking fee. Consumer robots have no restocking fee.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of the return arriving at the warehouse.
