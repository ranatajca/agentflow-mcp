# Support service levels

## Response times
Standard support replies within 2 business days. Loyalty members get a reply within 1 business day.
Professional customers with an active warranty get a same-day phone callback on business days.

## Repair turnaround
In-warranty repairs ship back within 10 business days of the robot arriving. Out-of-warranty repairs take up to 15 business days.

## Loyalty program
The Loyalty program is free and open to anyone who has registered at least one robot.
